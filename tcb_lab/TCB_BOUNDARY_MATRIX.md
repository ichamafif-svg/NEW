# Campaign IV — TCB boundary matrix (provisional, not architecture)

**2026-10-09.** Operationalized from the already frozen semantic scope in [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md). **This is a classification of duties, NOT a claim that each guarantee is experimentally validated.** A physical enforcement dependency belongs to the *effective trusted base* even when not implemented by the pure kernel. Terminology: **K**=deterministic kernel decision; **T**=indispensable trusted external mechanism; **U**=untrusted replaceable agent/tool. The boundary may have **K+T** combined responsibility. `HIGH` means unresolved empirical claims, not severity confirmed.

## Freeze rule

`KERNEL_REQUIRED`, `EXTERNAL_REQUIRED`, and `REPLACEABLE` are **non-exclusive**: most guarantees require a pure K rule PLUS a trusted physical T enforcement edge, while U produces inputs without constitutional authority. Do not interpret EXTERNAL_REQUIRED as outside the effective TCB. A limitation is *established* only when two indistinguishable kernel inputs lead to different physical truths **or** a trace proves a dependency indispensable, with assumptions and a counterfactual specified. `SCOPE_FREEZE_REVIEW.md` controls evidence classification; no scope edit is authorized here.
